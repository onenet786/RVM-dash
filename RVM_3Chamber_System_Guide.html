Electro-Mechanical Chamber Specifications

Chamber 1 • PET Plastic

Height-Based Multi-Tier Bottle Sizing

Serial Output: SIZE:<SIZE>;MATERIAL:PLASTIC

Workflow Execution

1
Entrance Detection:

Top ultrasonic (Trig: 9 / Echo: 10) detects object when distance drops ≥ 5cm for 3 cycles. Emits PLASTIC:OBJECT_DETECTED.

2
Iris Gate Actuation:

Upper iris servo (Pin 11) swings open from 10° to 120°. Bottle drops into the sizing chamber.

3
3-Level Ultrasonic Sizing:

Inside the sizing column, 3 ultrasonics sample item height (Bottom, Middle, Top) with 30ms anti-cross-talk spacing.

4
Drop & Discharge:

Upper iris closes (10°). Bottom drop gate (Pin 12) opens (120°), drops the bottle into bin, verifies chamber is empty for 5 cycles, then sends BOTTLE:CLEARED.

Sizing Classification Matrix

SMALL (≤ 250ml)
Bottom: ON Middle: OFF Top: OFF
MEDIUM (500ml)
Bottom: ON Middle: ON Top: OFF
LARGE (1.5L+)
Bottom: ON Middle: ON Top: ON
Chamber 2 • Beverage Cans

Sizing & Inductive Material Verification

Serial Output: SIZE:<SIZE>;MATERIAL:CAN (or REJECT)

Can Verification Sequence

1
Can Entry:

Entrance ultrasonic (Trig: 25 / Echo: 26) detects item. Iris servo (Pin 27) opens 120°.

2
Inductive Proximity Sampling:

Inductive sensor (Pin 32) samples 12 times over 180ms. Must register $\ge 9$ LOW states to confirm genuine metal.

3
Drop Gate:

Discharges via Servo (Pin 28) into the metal receptacle.

Sensor Envelope Details

• Entrance Servo: Pin 27
• Drop Gate Servo: Pin 28
• Inductive Proximity Pin: Pin 32 (INPUT_PULLUP)
• Sizing Sensors: 29/30, 31/44, 45/46

Chamber 3 • Paper Wastage

HX711 Load Cell Mass Measurement

Serial Output: SIZE:WEIGHT;MATERIAL:PAPER;WEIGHT_KG:0.185

Weighing & Discharge Cycle

1
Entry & Chute Arrival:

Top ultrasonic (33/34) opens Iris (Pin 35). Bottom ultrasonic (39/40) confirms paper rests on tray.

2
HX711 Reading (Pins 37 & 38):

Must exceed 20g threshold. Averages 8 readings after 3 consecutive stable samples ($\pm 5\text{ g}$).

3
Discharge:

Paper drop servo (Pin 36) dumps paper, verifying tare drops below 8g.
