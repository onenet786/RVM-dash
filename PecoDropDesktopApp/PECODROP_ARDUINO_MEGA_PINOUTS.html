Power Bus (+5V) VCC Logic 5V Pin Only for Logic ICs & Sensors (< 400mA)
Power Bus (GND) Common Ground GND Pins Must tie to 12V/6V PSU Grounds!
⚠️ Crucial Wiring Cautions:
1. Inductive Sensor (Pin 32): Industrial proximity sensors run on 12V–24V. Always pass signal through an optocoupler or resistive voltage divider to protect Pin 32 from exceeding 5.0V.
2. Servo Power Bus: Never power the 6 servo motors from Arduino 5V pin. Use a dedicated 5V/6V 10A DC supply with tied grounds.
