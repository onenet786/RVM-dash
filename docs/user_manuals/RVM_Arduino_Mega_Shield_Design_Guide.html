🎯 Mechanical & Form Factor Alignment

The PCB matches the exact footprint of the Arduino Mega 2560 Rev3. Female header receptacles on the bottom side mate firmly over the Mega pins, while the 4× M3 mounting holes align with the enclosure standoffs to prevent vibration fatigue.

  • Header Clearance: 11mm stacking height clears ATmega2560 MCU and USB Type-B jack.
  • M3 Standoffs: 4x non-plated chassis holes at exact Arduino Rev3 coordinates.
  • High-Current Edge: Screw terminals located on the left edge for shortest wiring harness to 12V PSU and 10A buck converter.
🛡️ Industrial Noise Immunity & Failsafes

Commercial RVM kiosks operate in high EMI environments with continuous mechanical cycling. The board incorporates master-tier hardening techniques:

  • Star Ground Topology: PGND (motors) and DGND (logic) isolate high motor switching currents from sensor lines.
  • TVS Back-EMF Diode: Clamps inductive flyback voltage spikes from 6 MG996R motors.
  • Optocoupled LJ12A3 Sensor: Eliminates 12V back-feed risk to the ATmega2560 microcontroller.

⚡ Dual-Rail Power Distribution Architecture

To ensure zero ATmega2560 brownout watchdog resets, the board separates the motor power domain from the digital logic domain:

RAIL 1: +5V_SERVO (TB_SERVO_PWR)
• Voltage: 5.0V – 5.2V DC (Dedicated 10A Buck)
• Max Transient Current: 15.0A (6x Servos in simultaneous stall)
• Protection: SMBJ6.0A TVS + 3x 1000µF Low-ESR Bulk Caps

RAIL 2: +5V_LOGIC (TB_5V_LOGIC)
• Voltage: 5.0V DC (Clean 3A Buck)
• Continuous Current: ~0.85A (Mega2560 + 8x HC-SR04 + HX711)
• Filtering: Ferrite Bead BLM21PG + 100µF Tantalum + 100nF MLCC

🔧 Assembly, Quality Control & Field Commissioning Checklist

Mandatory step-by-step verification checklist for mechatronics technicians before deploying the shield into commercial kiosk cabinets:

1. Cold Impedance & Polarity Verification

Verify resistance between +5V_SERVO and PGND is > 10 kΩ. Confirm TVS diode (D1) and electrolytic caps (C1-C3) are not in reverse orientation.

2. Bench Power Supply Smoke Test (Unseated from Mega)

Apply 12V to TB_12V, 5.1V to TB_SERVO_PWR, and 5.0V to TB_5V_LOGIC. Verify Green Logic LED and Blue Servo LED illuminate steadily with < 50mA idle current.

3. Optocoupler Isolation Pulse Test

Ground TB_INDUCTIVE SIG pin. Verify Amber Inductive LED illuminates and voltage at testpoint TP_IND_SIG drops to < 0.2V.

4. Mating & M3 Standoff Rigidity Check

Seat shield firmly onto Arduino Mega 2560 Rev3. Tighten 4x M3 hex standoffs with nylon isolation washers. Verify zero mechanical wobble.

5. Live Serial Firmware Baseline Calibration

Connect USB to Kiosk PC at 115200 baud. Send CALIBRATE command. Verify all 8 ultrasonic sensors return clean baselines and the HX711 returns 0.0g tare.
