Part A – Using PecoDrop

For employees, students and visitors of the host organization.

1. What PecoDrop is

PecoDrop is the enterprise edition of the ISP reverse vending machine. It is installed inside corporate campuses and accepts three materials, each through its own illuminated, shaped opening. Every accepted item is counted, converted into reward points and reported to your organization's sustainability dashboard in real time. Points are credited to the recycler's account, and the organization can turn them into recognition, cafeteria discounts, internal perks and CSR / ESG reporting.

Never insert: hands or tools, liquids, glass, batteries, aerosols, electronics, sharp metal, food, wet or contaminated paper, or documents that your information-security policy does not allow to be disposed of. PecoDrop is not a shredder; follow your organization's document-destruction policy before recycling paper.

2. Know the kiosk

2.1 Two landscape displays

  • Interactive kiosk display – shows the welcome header with the machine ID, the hardware diagnostics strip, the "HOW TO USE PECO DROP" guide and instruction video, the touchless start QR code, the Session Details panel (reward balance and the three material cards) and, on the right-hand pane, the LEADERBOARD • LIVE RANKINGS with the podium and a "RECENT" activity ticker.
  • Digital signage display – plays the organization's advertisement video playlist ("PECO DROP / DIGITAL SIGNAGE") while the kiosk is idle and during sessions.

Which physical screen is on the left or right is configured per site by the operator (see section 16). The factory default places the interactive kiosk display on the left and the signage display on the right.

Figure 1 – Interactive display at the welcome state: header and diagnostics (top left), how-to-use guide with "SCAN TO START" QR (centre), Session Details (bottom left) and the live leaderboard (right). This capture was made in demo mode with the hardware unplugged, so the compartment cards read "unavailable"; on a production kiosk they read Ready.

2.2 The console

  • Keypad – a numeric keypad. Employees only need 0 (start), Enter (finish and claim) and the digits 1–5 for the experience rating. * or Esc cancels the current dialog.
  • Touch – touching the glowing "TOUCH SCREEN OR PRESS 0" card has the same effect as pressing 0.
  • Touchless start QR – the white "📱 TOUCHLESS • SCAN TO START" card on screen. Scan it with the Trash to Cash app (or the phone camera) to start a session that is already linked to your account, so the points are credited automatically when you finish.

2.3 Session Details panel

Below the guide area, the SESSION DETAILS panel shows YOUR REWARD BALANCE (points earned in this session) with the REDEEM button, and four cards: PLASTIC (S / M / L counts and total pieces), CAN (S / M / L and total), PAPER (weight in kg and total) and REJ (rejected items). The counts update the moment an item is accepted.

3. Before you start

  • The header shows a green LIVE badge and the correct machine ID for your site (for example PECO-LHR-01).
  • The diagnostics strip reads HARDWARE: COMx (green), DB: OK and API: OK, and the machine status reads MACHINE: IDLE or READY.
  • The three material cards show Ready. A card reading Full or Unavailable means that material cannot be accepted right now.
  • Your bottle or can is empty, uncrushed enough to keep its shape, and your paper is dry, loose (no binders, clips, plastic sleeves) and approved for disposal.
  • Have the Trash to Cash app ready, or know the mobile number registered to your account.

5. Depositing plastic bottles – ⭕ Circle aperture

  1. Empty the PET bottle. Keep the cap on or off, it does not matter, but the bottle must keep a recognisable shape.
  2. Check that the PLASTIC card reads Ready.
  3. Insert one bottle into the magenta circle opening and let go. The screen shows "Plastic bottle detected" then the OPTICAL SIZING CHAMBER overlay ("Analyzing Small (S) • Medium (M) • Large (L) – Please keep hands away from the intake aperture while measuring").
  4. Wait. The kiosk measures the bottle, opens the drop gate and shows "ITEM ACCEPTED! • Points credited to your session • Ready for next item" with the size, material and points (for example "MEDIUM PLASTIC – 10 points"). A short celebration animation plays.
  5. Confirm that the PLASTIC count and YOUR REWARD BALANCE increased, then insert the next item.
Figure 3 – Plastic bottle accepted: the PLASTIC card and the reward balance have increased.

6. Depositing cans – 🔺 Triangle aperture

  1. Use an empty aluminium beverage can. Aerosol cans, food tins and batteries are not accepted.
  2. Check that the CAN card reads Ready.
  3. Insert one can into the green triangle opening and release it. The screen shows "Can detected — verifying metal" and the sizing overlay.
  4. Wait for "ITEM ACCEPTED!" with the size and points, then check the CAN count.
Figure 4 – Can accepted.

7. Depositing office paper – 🟦 Square aperture (weighed)

Paper is not counted in pieces. A digital load cell (HX711) under the square intake weighs the paper in grams and converts the weight into points according to your organization's "per kg" rule.

  1. Confirm the paper is permitted by your information-security policy. Remove binders, clips, plastic folders, batteries, food and anything wet.
  2. Check that the PAPER card reads Ready.
  3. Place the paper into the cyan square opening as shown in the instruction video. The screen shows "Paper detected — preparing scale" and then "WEIGHING PAPER IN PROGRESS... – Please leave paper on the scale until measurement stabilizes".
  4. Do not push, hold or add paper while the measurement is running.
  5. When the weight is stable the gate opens and the screen shows "Paper: 250 g (0.250 kg) – 4 points" (example values). The PAPER card shows the cumulative weight in kg.
Figure 5 – Paper accepted: the PAPER card reports 0.250 kg and the reward balance updated.

9. Finishing the session and claiming points

When you have inserted your last item press Enter or tap REDEEM. The guards you may see: "Start recycling first", "No items to credit / Insert an item before using your wallet", or "Wallet unavailable / Connect local DB or Central Network before crediting a wallet" (the kiosk is fully offline; ask the operator).

9.1 If you started with the touchless QR (account already known)

The points are credited immediately and the ⭐ EXPERIENCE RATING window appears: "✓ Wallet Credited: 03xx xxxxxxx (+25 PTS) – HOW WAS YOUR EXPERIENCE? – Press 1 to 5 on keypad or tap stars to rate · Press ENTER to finish – Auto-completing in 8s...". Press 1–5 then Enter, or tap Submit Rating ✓, or simply wait; the default rating is 5 stars. Skip (or Esc) finishes without rating.

9.2 If you started with touch or key 0 (anonymous session)

The 💳 WALLET TRANSFER · Scan QR or Enter Mobile Number to Claim Points window appears with the RECYCLED DEPOSIT SUMMARY (items recycled, ⭕ Plastic / 🔺 Cans / 🟦 Paper breakdown and POINTS TO CLAIM). Choose one of two ways to claim:

  1. Scan the claim QR ("📲 SCAN QR CODE TO CLAIM POINTS – Scan using RVM Mobile App or your phone camera") with Trash to Cash or the phone camera, or type the SESSION CODE (FOR MANUAL APP ENTRY) shown under the QR (format PECO-LHR-01_3a7698c8) into the app. The window shows "Waiting for scan..." and then "✓ Claimed by <your name>!". The QR is valid for 90 seconds.
  2. Enter your mobile number in "📱 CITIZEN MOBILE WALLET NUMBER – Enter 11 digits starting with 03" and press Claim Points ➔ (or Enter). The field validates as you type ("✓ Valid mobile number" / "⚠ Invalid number: Must start with 03…"). Points are credited to the account registered with that number; if the number has no account yet, one is created and the points wait there until you register in the app with the same number.

After a successful claim the kiosk shows "THANK YOU FOR RECYCLING! – Your wallet has been credited and feedback recorded.", asks for the 1–5 star rating, and returns to the welcome screen.

If you walk away: once at least one item has been accepted and nothing happens for 30 seconds, the status shows "Session Closing in 10s…". After 40 seconds a QR-authenticated session is auto-credited to the signed-in account; an anonymous session shows "Session Expired" and the points are not credited to anyone. Pressing ✖ Close Session, Esc or * in the wallet window also closes the session without claiming.

10. Points, rewards and your organization's programme

  • How points are calculated. Plastic and cans earn points per piece by size; paper earns points per kilogram. The values are set by ISP and your organization on the Master Portal and pushed to the kiosk automatically. Factory defaults when no rule has been configured: plastic S / M / L = 5 / 10 / 15 points, cans S / M / L = 10 / 15 / 20 points, paper = 15 points per kg. Your kiosk may use different values; the points shown on screen at acceptance time are always the ones that apply.
  • Where the points go. Into the recycler account identified at claim time (QR login or mobile number). The same account is used by the Trash to Cash app, where you can see your balance, session history and redeem rewards.
  • Session summary. The kiosk can show "Items Recycled / Points Earned / CO₂ Saved / Water Saved" for the session (0.15 kg CO₂ and 0.75 L water per item are the on-kiosk estimates).
  • Corporate programme. Departmental rankings, cafeteria discounts, internal perks, and CSR / ESG reports are managed by your organization's administrator on the Master Portal; the kiosk only records the deposits and credits the points. If a credit is missing, give your administrator the machine ID, the time and the points shown on screen.
  • Leaderboard. The right-hand pane ranks the top recyclers of your organization. Ranking is by points balance; it refreshes automatically and after every credited session.

Part B – Operating and maintaining PecoDrop

For site operators, facility staff and ISP technicians. Contains confidential key sequences.

Confidential. Sections 13, 14, 19 and 22 describe key sequences that can stop the machine, open service controls or power off the kiosk computer. Do not post Part B where the public can read it.

11. Daily opening and closing checklist

Opening

  • Both displays are on, full-screen and in the expected physical positions (section 16).
  • The application started (it launches automatically; otherwise run launch-kiosk.ps1 or the PecoDropDesktopApp executable). Wait for the firmware handshake: the status passes through "Calibrating... / Keep pipe empty" and ends with HARDWARE: COMx green and MACHINE: IDLE.
  • Diagnostics strip shows DB: OK and API: OK and the header badge is LIVE. "NO NETWORK" means the central API is unreachable (section 18.4).
  • PLASTIC, CAN and PAPER cards read Ready; bins are empty and seated; the paper scale is empty.
  • QR scanner window, keypad, screens and the three illuminated apertures are clean and undamaged; nothing is left inside an aperture.
  • The machine ID and location in the header are correct for the site.
  • Where site policy allows, run one test deposit and confirm it appears on the Master Portal.

Closing

  • Let any active session finish (or press 77 to stop it, see section 13).
  • Check all apertures and the return path for stuck items.
  • Empty bins that read Full; note any warning banner for the maintenance log.
  • Follow the site's always-on or shutdown policy (section 22). Do not switch off at the wall while a session is running.

18. Point rules, leaderboard and data sync with the Master Portal

18.1 Point rules

Order of precedence: Master Portal machine config (fetched with every heartbeat) → local dbo.PointSettings → built-in defaults (plastic 5 / 10 / 15, cans 10 / 15 / 20, paper 15 per kg). Paper rules support per-kg, per-gram and per-piece units; points are rounded incrementally on the cumulative weight of the session.

18.2 Heartbeat (every 15 s)

POST /api/machine/heartbeat with machine ID, status (ONLINE, IN_SESSION, BIN_FULL, WARNING, ERROR), fill levels of the three bins, scale status, smoke flag, anti-cheat trips, local IP, location and coordinates. The portal's Machine Health tab shows this data; a kiosk that misses heartbeats is shown OFFLINE.

18.3 Sessions

Each accepted item is posted live to /api/machine/sync-session with the cumulative counts of the session; the final post carries the claiming user. Feedback ratings go to /api/machine/feedback. The leaderboard is read from /api/usernames scoped to the organization the kiosk is bound to (corporate kiosks never show public citizens).

18.4 Offline behaviour

Deposits and wallet credits continue with the local SQL Server when the portal is unreachable (header shows NO NETWORK). Unsynced rows are re-sent automatically on the 15-second cycle and can be pushed manually from the Security tab. If both the local DB and the portal are down, the kiosk refuses to open the wallet window ("Wallet unavailable").

19. Hardware and firmware (Arduino Mega 2560)

19.1 Overview

  • Firmware: PecoDropDesktopApp\Arduino\Peco_Arduino\Peco_Arduino.ino, board "Arduino Mega or Mega 2560 / ATmega2560", 115200 baud. Full pin sheet: PECODROP_ARDUINO_MEGA_PINOUTS.html.
  • Each chamber has an entrance ultrasonic, an iris servo, a drop-gate servo and sizing sensors (three ultrasonics per bottle/can chamber: bottom + middle + top = Large). The paper chamber has top and bottom ultrasonics plus the HX711 load cell. Optical bin-full sensors on pins 47–49; an MQ-6 gas sensor reports MQ6:WARNING / CLEAR.

19.2 Host lease (the firmware never runs without the application)

  • At power-up the firmware moves all servos to the closed safe state and prints HOST:WAITING. It accepts only STOP, RESET and STATUS until the application sends HOST:ALIVE (answered by HOST:ONLINE) and HOST:START.
  • The application renews the lease every 1.5 s. If no renewal arrives for 8 s the firmware stops, un-calibrates, closes all servos and prints HOST:TIMEOUT / MACHINE:STOPPED. This is why the machine goes idle automatically when the application closes or the USB cable is removed.

19.3 Calibration

  • Triggered automatically at connection and after a reconnect, or manually with 99 / Calibrate All. The application retries a failed calibration 6 times at 8 s, then every 15 s.
  • The firmware waits for the bin sensors to settle (up to 2.5 s, requiring 750 ms all-clear), measures the empty-chamber distances of every enabled sensor, tares the scale and reports CALIBRATION:OK followed by MACHINE:STARTED / MACHINE:IDLE. A compartment that fails is removed from the intake loop and recovers automatically later; calibration succeeds if at least one compartment is good.
  • Bin-full sensing in the current firmware build: the optical bin sensors on pins 47–49 are compiled out (the *_BIN_SENSOR_ENABLED flags are false in Peco_Arduino.ino), so compartments do not automatically report Full. Operators must check bin levels visually; the dashboard fill figures come from the kiosk's own counters. Enable the flags and re-upload the firmware to activate sensor-based detection.
  • Bin-full reports received before CALIBRATION:OK are ignored, which prevents false "Full" states at start-up. Failure messages on screen: "Hardware calibration required – Empty all compartments, check ultrasonic wiring, then calibrate" or "Check HX711 wiring and load-cell power, then calibrate".

19.4 Iris enable/disable and sensor bypass (service features)

  • Iris control (IRIS:<PLASTIC|METAL|PAPER|ALL>:ENABLE|DISABLE, hotkeys 620–629 or Admin panel): a disabled chamber keeps its iris closed, is reported Unavailable and ignores objects. Use it to take one chamber out of service without stopping the kiosk.
  • Sensor bypass (BYPASS:<PLASTIC_MID|PLASTIC_TOP|METAL_MID|METAL_TOP|BIN>:ENABLE|DISABLE, Admin panel): a bypassed sizing transducer is replaced by its empty-chamber baseline so the chamber keeps working with reduced sizing accuracy; bypassing the bin sensors disables bin-full detection. Bypass flags persist in config.txt and are re-applied at each calibration. Restore the sensor as soon as it is repaired.

19.5 USB hot-plug and reconnection

The application scans the serial ports every 1.5 s. It prefers the configured ArduinoPort, otherwise the single USB-serial device found (COM1 and the camera port are excluded). On reconnection it renews the lease, requests calibration and waits for CALIBRATION:OK before accepting bin-full states. While disconnected the screen shows "HARDWARE: OFFLINE – Cable Disconnected – Arduino cable unplugged — reconnect cable to resume".

19.6 Uploading new firmware

  1. Close PecoDrop first so the COM port is free.
  2. In the Arduino IDE select "Arduino Mega or Mega 2560", processor ATmega2560, the kiosk's COM port, open Peco_Arduino.ino and Upload.
  3. Errors such as "stk500v2_getsync()" or "can't open device COMx" are port / bootloader / cable problems, not sketch-size problems: check the cable, the port number and that no other program holds the port.
  4. Restart PecoDrop; it must show HOST:WAITING → calibration → HARDWARE: COMx.
  5. The scale can be tested standalone with Arduino\HX711_Test\HX711_Test.ino and the SCALE command in the Serial Monitor.

21. Cleaning, privacy and emergencies

  • Use screen-safe cleaner on a cloth; never spray liquids into the apertures, the QR scanner window or the keypad. No metal tools inside the apertures.
  • Handle recovered office paper under the organization's document-security policy. Never photograph or share employee QR codes or mobile numbers shown on the telemetry overlay (55).
  • Smoke, burning smell, exposed wiring, liquid ingress, repeated uncontrolled gate movement, broken glass or forced cabinet access: isolate the kiosk, switch it off at the mains and call ISP support.

22. Restart, shutdown and exiting the application

  • 1218 opens "Are you sure you want to RESTART the kiosk computer?" – confirm with Enter / 1 ("Yes, Restart") or cancel with 0 / Backspace.
  • 1219 opens the same dialog for SHUT DOWN. Both run a controlled Windows restart / shutdown; the firmware falls back to its safe state when the host lease expires.
  • Esc at the idle screen exits the kiosk application to the Windows desktop (closes the signage window and stops the heartbeat). Use this only for maintenance, then relaunch the application.
