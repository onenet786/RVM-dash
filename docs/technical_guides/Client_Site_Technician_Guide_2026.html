1. Purpose and permitted work

This guide is for trained client personnel responsible for opening checks, cleaning, bin servicing, basic recovery, approved demonstrations and reporting faults. It covers both ISP machine families without mixing their material paths or workflows.

Public RVM

Installed at malls, transit stations and public locations. Accepts plastic bottles, beverage cans and UBC/Tetra Pak cartons; rejected objects use the reject path. Sessions may provide wallet, mobile-load or coupon rewards.

Enterprise PecoDrop

Corporate dual-display kiosk with QR scanner and keypad. Its apertures are ⭕ magenta plastic, 🔺 green metal/cans and 🟦 cyan weighed office paper.

Stop-work boundary: Do not open electrical panels, bypass interlocks, work near moving gates, change wiring/serial ports, upload firmware or use manufacturer servo-test codes. Isolate power and contact ISP when physical access to an actuator or energized compartment is required.

Before touching the kiosk

□Floor is dry, machine is stable, vents and emergency access are clear.
□No person, hand or object is inside an intake aperture or discharge path.
□Bins are installed correctly and are not overfilled.
□Displays, scanner/keypad, intake openings and visible cables are undamaged.
□Record machine ID, location, date/time and the exact on-screen message before recovery.

2. Approved operational codes

Enter a numeric sequence on the physical keyboard/keypad as one uninterrupted buffer sequence. Do not add spaces. Use only when the screen is responsive and all intake paths are clear.

Keyboard-only shortcuts: S stops a session and A opens administrator login on both apps. On PecoDrop, F2 or D opens demo testing. These are for an attached service keyboard and should not be exposed during normal public use.
Do not use Esc as routine shutdown. It can close the kiosk application after diagnostics are recorded. Use 1218 for restart or 1219 for controlled shutdown.

Manufacturer-only controls: PecoDrop actuator/servo test codes 601–612 are intentionally not provided in this client guide. They can move iris and drop gates independently of normal intake. Only ISP manufacturer technicians may use them under a controlled service procedure.

PecoDrop calibration/start sequence

  1. Confirm plastic, metal and paper apertures are empty; fit all bins correctly.
  2. Start the PecoDrop application and allow the controller to reconnect.
  3. If calibration is not automatically confirmed, enter 99 once.
  4. Wait for a successful calibration/ready indication. Do not insert test material while calibration is running.
  5. Enter 66, then perform one supervised test for each enabled material path.

4. Opening, cleaning and bin service

Daily opening procedure

  1. Inspect the enclosure, floor, plugs, visible cables, intakes and bins.
  2. Power on approved external equipment, then the kiosk computer/machine according to the site procedure.
  3. Confirm the correct application opens full-screen and the correct machine identity appears.
  4. For PecoDrop, verify both landscape displays and their assigned roles; correct them with 0012 or 0021 if necessary.
  5. Check network status and open telemetry with 888 if any connection indicator is abnormal.
  6. Run one clean, valid sample per material path. Remove all samples from bins afterward if site policy requires it.

Cleaning and emptying

  • End the active session. On PecoDrop, use 77 before permitted bin service.
  • Use a dry microfiber cloth on screens and scanner glass. Apply cleaner to the cloth, never directly to electronics.
  • Do not push tools through an aperture or force any gate. Do not spray water or solvent inside the machine.
  • Empty and refit bins without disturbing sensors, wiring or load-cell platforms. PecoDrop paper weighing surfaces must remain free of trapped material.
  • Close and latch all client-access doors; then restore operation and perform a supervised test.
Paper handling: PecoDrop office paper is measured by weight. Do not lean on, store tools on or manually press the paper platform; this can corrupt the zero/calibration reference.

Controlled restart

  1. End the user session and record the fault.
  2. Confirm all intake paths are clear.
  3. Enter 1218 and select Yes in the confirmation dialog.
  4. Allow the application and controller to recover without repeated key presses.
  5. Verify ready state and complete a supervised material test.

Controlled shutdown

  1. End the session, stop public access and complete bin/service records.
  2. Enter 1219 and select Yes.
  3. Wait until Windows and the computer are fully off before switching approved external power.

6. Demo and training use

  1. Place the kiosk out of public service and ensure no real user session is active.
  2. Enter 1122. Confirm the Demo Mode indicator/testing window appears.
  3. Use only the simulator functions needed for the training scenario. Demo events are not proof of physical sensor or payout operation.
  4. Close demo testing, restart the kiosk with 1218 if required by site procedure, and verify normal mode before public use.
Never leave Demo Mode available to customers. Do not use simulated acceptance to certify hardware repair.

7. Information required for ISP support

Send one complete fault package. Accurate evidence is faster and safer than repeated resets.

□Machine ID, machine type (RVM or PecoDrop), client/site and exact location.
□Date/time, operator name and whether a customer session/reward was active.
□Exact screen message plus clear photos/video of the symptom; do not include customer personal data.
□Telemetry/status shown through 888 and current network condition.
□Material/path involved and the last successful operation.
□Actions already taken, in order, including codes used and restart outcome.
□Any noise, odor, heat, liquid ingress, impact damage or power event.

8. Return-to-service authorization

□No exposed wiring, liquid, heat, odor, structural damage or unsafe movement.
□All client-access doors/bins are correctly installed and secured.
□Application is full-screen, machine identity is correct and telemetry is normal.
□PecoDrop calibration is confirmed; both displays and all three material identities are correct.
□Each enabled intake passes one supervised valid test and rejects an unsuitable test item correctly.
□Session completes correctly and any reward flow is confirmed or intentionally disabled.
□Fault and corrective action are entered in the site log.
